# School Platform Changelog

## 2026-09-23 — the ops desks

- Fees: term charges (school-wide or per class), receipts in cash,
  transfer, POS or other, balances and a debtors list. Kobo-integer
  ledger, naira-string inputs.
- ID cards: session-scoped cards with REN-<year>-NNNNNN serials,
  single and batch issue, revoke/reactivate, printable card sheet with
  print-exact black on white faces.
- Timetable: weekly grid editor per class, subjects or labelled blocks
  per period, whole-week saves in one transaction.
- Exam bank: original starter questions per subject per term (pour in
  one tap), school-written questions, published papers that draw from
  the pool at print time.
- Scheme drafts: NERDC-aligned weekly scheme of work poured into every
  empty syllabus slot for a session.
- Offline pack: the staff app's school pack now carries the exam bank
  with a tap-to-reveal reader for classroom oral quizzes.
- Scheme drafts: one tap drafts the NERDC weekly scheme into every
  empty syllabus slot; teachers edit the draft, never a blank page.
- Search everywhere: exam pool and debtor list filter live, and CSV
  exports carry the attendance file to the office.
- Content doctrine: classnotes.ng harvest postponed; flashlearners and
  edudelight carry the weekly rotation. See docs/content-sourcing.md.

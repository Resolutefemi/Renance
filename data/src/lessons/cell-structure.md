## The cell is the unit of life

Every living thing is built from cells, and every cell carries the machinery
of life: taking in nutrients, releasing energy, removing waste and
reproducing. The cell theory states that all living organisms are made of
cells, the cell is the basic unit of structure and function, and new cells
arise only from existing cells.

- Robert Hooke first saw cells in cork in 1665
- Schwann and Schleiden extended the idea to all living things
- Virchow showed that cells come only from pre-existing cells

> Key point: examiners love the cell theory statement, so learn all three parts as one sentence each.

## Organelles and their jobs

The **nucleus** houses chromosomes and controls all cell activity through
DNA. The **mitochondrion** releases energy from glucose during aerobic
respiration, which is why metabolically active cells are packed with them.
**Ribosomes** build proteins and are the smallest organelles you are
required to know.

- Chloroplasts trap light energy for photosynthesis; they contain chlorophyll
- The cell membrane is partially permeable and controls what enters and leaves
- The sap vacuole keeps plant cells firm by storing water and dissolved substances
- Lysosomes digest worn-out organelles and invading microbes

### Plant versus animal cells

Plant cells carry a rigid **cellulose cell wall**, chloroplasts and a large
sap vacuole; animal cells have none of these three. That trio is the cleanest
way to answer any "state two differences" question.

> Key point: cell wall, chloroplasts, large vacuole are present in plants and absent in animals.

## Levels of organisation

Cells specialise, then cooperate. Similar cells form a **tissue**, different
tissues form an **organ**, organs working together form a **system**, and all
the systems together make the **organism**. In the exam, trace the ladder in
order; a common trap is jumping from cell straight to organ.

- Cell -> muscle cell; tissue -> muscle tissue; organ -> heart; system -> circulatory system
- Amoeba and Chlamydomonas are unicellular: one cell does every job

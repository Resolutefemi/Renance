Which of the following organelles is primarily responsible for the synthesis of lipids and detoxification of harmful substances in a eukaryotic cell?

check_circle Correct!

The Smooth Endoplasmic Reticulum (SER) is a network of membrane-bound tubules and sacs. It is a major site of lipid synthesis, including phospholipids and cholesterol, and also plays a crucial role in detoxifying drugs and poisons, particularly in liver cells.

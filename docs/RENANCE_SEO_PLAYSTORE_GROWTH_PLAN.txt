4. THE SECRET WEAPON: GOOGLE QUIZ RICH CARDS (POSITION 0)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Google recently introduced a dedicated search feature specifically for education
called "schema.org/Quiz".

When we tag our pages with this schema, Google will display interactive quiz 
cards directly on the Google search results page before the student even clicks 
a link. This guarantees Renance ranks above Myschool.ng and Pass.ng.

Furthermore, when students ask AI assistants (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google Gemini) 
to solve a JAMB or WAEC question, the AI will pull our step-by-step explanations 
and explicitly cite: "Source: Renance.app".

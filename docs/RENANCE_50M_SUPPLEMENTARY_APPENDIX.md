### 2.2 Why Renance Works 100% Offline (Digital Inclusion for All Students)
Most modern educational apps fail in Nigeria because they require constant 4G/5G data connections. A student in a rural or semi-urban area whose parents cannot afford daily data bundles is shut out.

Renance solves this through an **Offline-First Architecture**:
- **One-Time Sync:** A student connects to Wi-Fi or a hotspot for just 3 to 5 minutes to download a subject pack (about 12MB, smaller than a 3-minute video).
- **Months of Offline Study:** Once downloaded, the student can turn on **Airplane Mode** and practice hundreds of full CBT mock exams, review past questions, read study notes, and track their daily study streaks for months without spending a single Kobo on data.
- **Built for Cheap Phones:** The app is specifically programmed and tested on 2GB RAM phones (like Tecno Pop, Infinix Smart, and Itel). It takes up very little phone memory (under 48MB) so it never freezes, overheats, or causes the phone to hang.

### 2.4 The CBT Exam Clock Coach (How It Stops Exam Hall Panic)
In JAMB UTME, a student must answer **180 questions across 4 subjects in 120 minutes** (7,200 seconds). That gives an average of exactly **40 seconds per question**.

When a student spends 4 minutes fighting with one tough Maths or Chemistry question, their brain panics, their heart races, and they run out of time—leaving 30 or 40 easy questions unanswered at the end.

The **Renance CBT Clock Coach** solves this during mock exams:
1. **Intelligent Timing per Question Type:**
   - Quick recall questions (like Biology vocabulary or English synonyms) should take only **25 seconds**.
   - Standard understanding questions (like Commerce or Literature) take **36 seconds**.
   - Difficult calculation questions (like Physics or Further Maths) are given up to **55 seconds**.
2. **Friendly Ambient Visual Gauge:**
   - **`[ON TRACK]` (Green Light):** The student is moving at a winning speed.
   - **`[LINGERING]` (Yellow Light):** The student has spent more than 45 seconds on one question. The gauge pulses gently: *"You are lingering here. Pick your best guess and keep moving so you don't lose time."*
   - **`[TIME TRAP RISK]` (Red Light):** The student has spent over 75 seconds on a single question. A clear alert appears: *"Time trap alert! Do not sacrifice 40 easy questions at the end for this single question. Skip and move forward now!"*
3. **Post-Exam Panic Autopsy Report:**
   After the exam, Renance gives the student a clear report:
   - Shows the exact questions where they wasted too much time and still got it wrong.
   - Shows how many marks they lost at the end because the clock ran out.
   - Shows their projected score improvement if they had simply skipped those time-traps (students consistently gain **+28 to +44 extra marks** after just two weeks of Clock Coach training).

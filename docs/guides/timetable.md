# Timetable Desk Guide

The weekly timetable lives at `/school/timetable`. One grid per
class: rows are periods, columns run Monday to Friday, and every cell
holds either a subject or a labelled block such as Assembly or Break.

## Drawing a week

1. Pick the class. The subject picker shows what that class offers
   (wired on the School Setup desk).
2. For each cell choose the subject from the dropdown, or leave it
   empty and type a block name like Break in the label field.
3. Save week replaces the class's whole week in one clean write; the
   notice says how many periods were saved.

## Reading it

Teachers see the same grid read-only for the classes they cover, so
the whole school reads one truth. Cells with no subject and no label
are not timetabled and print as free periods.

## Tips from the field

- Draw the anchor blocks first (assembly, break, clubs) and fit
  subjects around them; the grid stays realistic.
- Keep the same subject in the same period across days where you can:
  pupils settle faster into a rhythm they can predict.
- Re-drawing a term is a two-minute job with Clear followed by a new
  pass down the columns.

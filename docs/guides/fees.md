# Fees Desk Guide

The fees desk is management's money window on the school portal. It
lives at `/school/fees` and has two tabs: Term charges and Student
balances. Teachers see the charges list read-only; the balances and
receipts stay with management.

## Pricing a term

1. Pick the term and session in the header. Everything below filters
   to that pick.
2. On the Term charges tab, fill the pricing card: choose Every class
   for a school-wide charge (PTA levy, sports levy) or one class for a
   class-only charge.
3. Type a title (for example School fees), an optional note, and the
   amount in naira. Decimals work: 2500.50 means two thousand five
   hundred naira, fifty kobo.
4. Save charge. Re-saving the same title for the same class, term and
   session updates the price instead of doubling it.

## Taking receipts

1. Switch to Student balances and filter to a class if needed.
2. Find the student and tap Receipt. The amount field pre-fills with
   their outstanding balance.
3. Pick the charge the money is for, choose the method (cash,
   transfer, POS or other), and add a reference for transfers.
4. Record. The balances tab and the totals cards refresh immediately.

## Reading the ledger

The three totals cards above the balances table show what the term
charges the visible students, what they have paid, and the gap. The
table lists each student's position with their last payment date.
Amounts display as naira with the naira sign; the ledger itself keeps
integer kobo so nothing rounds away.

## Rules worth knowing

- A charge that already has receipts cannot be removed. It stays so
  the payment history keeps its meaning.
- Deleting a charge is the only destructive action here; receipts are
  never deletable from the portal.
- School-wide charges hit every active student in the school,
  including those enrolled after the charge was priced.

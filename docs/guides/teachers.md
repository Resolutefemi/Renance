# Teachers Desk Guide

Staff accounts and their boundaries live at `/school/teachers`.

## Creating a teacher account

1. Fill name, email, password and an optional staff code.
2. The new member holds the teacher role and signs into the portal
   and the app like any user.

## Handing out classes and subjects

An assignment is a class+subject pair. Assign English in JSS 2 to one
teacher and Mathematics in SSS 1 to another; a teacher may hold many
pairs and a pair may be shared when two staff cover the same class.

Boundaries follow assignments everywhere: the teacher's syllabus desk
shows their subjects, the attendance register opens their classes,
and the results grid unlocks exactly their cells. Nothing else is
editable, which is the point.

## The app for staff

Teachers and management sign into the Renance app with the same
account and download their school pack: syllabus, scheme of work,
notes and the exam bank, readable offline in the classroom. Records,
fees, cards and enrollment stay on the web by design.

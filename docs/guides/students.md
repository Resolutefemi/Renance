# Students Desk Guide

Enrollment lives at `/school/students`. Every pupil is a record with
full details, stored per class, and every result, receipt and ID card
hangs off that record.

## Enrolling a pupil

1. Pick the class and use the enroll form: full name, admission
   number, sex and session are the core fields.
2. Open the pupil to complete the detail form: date of birth,
   guardian name and phone, address, photo and status.
3. Status moves a pupil through active, left and graduated without
   deleting history. Fees, results and attendance keep their rows.

## Subject tracks for senior classes

Primary and JSS pupils take everything their class offers. SSS
students offer a track: open the pupil, set their subjects from the
class list (science, art or commercial plus their core), and from then
on exactly those subjects appear on their result sheet. Change the
set any time before results finalize; the sheet follows the student,
not the class list.

## What hangs off a student

Results and report cards per term, fee balances and receipts, the ID
card serial, the attendance history and the result-check PIN all key
off the enrollment record. Enroll once, correctly, and the rest of
the portal inherits the work.

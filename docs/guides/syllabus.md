# Syllabus Desk Guide

The academic heart of the portal lives at `/school/syllabus`: per
class, per subject, per term, the scheme of work and the topics with
their notes.

## Reading a term

Pick a class and a subject. Each term card shows the weekly scheme
strip and the topic list. Open a topic to read or edit its note; save
writes it for the whole school.

## Drafting schemes

Draft empty schemes writes the NERDC-aligned weekly topics into every
term whose scheme is still blank for the session. It never overwrites
a scheme a teacher has already built. Teachers then edit objectives
and activities on the draft, which is faster than starting from a
stare.

## Pouring notes

For bulk content, the bulk-notes import (wired to the weekly harvest)
drops whole topic sets into a term with provenance per topic. The
same desk also hands teachers the PDF path: every topic's note
downloads as a clean black and white sheet for the classroom wall.

## Offline life

Everything here rides into the staff app's school pack: syllabus,
scheme, notes and now the exam bank, readable in class without
network. Changes on the web version the pack; the app refreshes it.

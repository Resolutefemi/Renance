# Result Checker Guide

The public checker lives at `/school/check`. No sign-in, no app, no
data bundle heavy lifting: it is one form and one result.

## For parents and students

1. Get the 6-digit PIN from your school (issued when the term's
   results finalize).
2. Enter the PIN, pick the term, and type the session exactly as the
   school writes it, for example 2025/2026.
3. Check result opens the full report card: subjects, scores, grades,
   position, class average, teacher and principal remarks, and the
   school logo. Download report card (PDF) gives you the printable
   copy.

## For schools

A PIN belongs to one student, one term, one session. Share it with the
family, not the class; it is a private key, not a serial number. If a
PIN leaks, re-finalizing the term rotates PINs for the whole class,
which is the documented trade-off for a credential with no password
field.
